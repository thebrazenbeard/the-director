## Frozen preregistration (before external literature lookup)

**Q-20261101:** Among unconventional but academically investigated models of reality, information, intelligence and consciousness, which offer an empirically discriminating, $0-accessible research question or reusable Director software capability, without assuming a creator or an outside-reality communication channel exists?

**H1 (testability):** At least three examined research families have operational measurements or open datasets on which rival ordinary models can be compared. Prediction: a cited primary method/dataset and a bounded local analysis can be specified for each. Falsifier: no independent measurement/data access or no defined rival-prediction difference.

**H2 (communication):** A public machine-readable invitation can be built with an ordinary two-way response route, but present physical science does not supply a verified addressing or authentication mechanism for a hypothetical outside-universe intelligence. Prediction: existing communications/technosignature literature defines measurable channels and false-positive controls, not an outside-universe protocol. Falsifier: independently replicated primary evidence of such an authenticated channel.

**H3 (agent continuity):** Reproducible software-state transfer is testable as state/behavior preservation, while personal subjective continuity is not established by state-hash or transcript equivalence alone. Prediction: literature identifies unresolved theories/empirical discriminators and no validated subjective-identity checksum. Falsifier: an independently validated operational subjective-continuity measure applicable to transfers.

**H4 (statistical restraint):** Any search across many science-inspired channels must include a frozen null, controls, independence graph, multiplicity correction and a stop rule. Prediction: reviewed empirical methods reveal ways that post-selection or shared sources create apparent discoveries. Falsifier: a method proving strong attribution without these controls across comparable search conditions.

**Methods (all exploratory literature evaluation; no external experiment is pre-claimed):**

- `M-LIT-01`: primary-source survey of foundations of computational/simulation hypotheses, fundamental physics and information-theoretic limitations.
- `M-LIT-02`: primary-source survey of physical signaling, SETI/technosignatures, randomness and testable communication protocols.

**Stopping rules:** stop a literature track when two suitable primary sources establish its current empirical ceiling or when direct-source access fails after two focused searches; do not exceed 12 high-level candidate tracks in this first pass. An undocumented source is an unanswered gap, not a disproof. Any new physical experiment must be separately preregistered before data collection. This first pass ends as **research-only**; it does not authorize equipment, software effects, public claims, host installation, or a GitHub merge.

## Ranked $0 execution packages (no experiment has run yet)

1. **EXP-SETI-NEG-01, first:** Reproduce the *classification method*, not the telescope. In a disposable synthetic signal corpus, plant ground-truth narrowband chirps, clock-harmonic interference and null noise. Freeze data split, threshold, grouping by originating emitter, look-elsewhere correction, and negative control *before* running. Report precision/recall and false-alert families. Only after this works consider a small published BLC1 filterbank excerpt and preserve observational origin.
2. **EXP-ALIFE-ABLAT-01:** Compare seeded, stdlib-only cellular automata under a novelty/complexity metric against shuffled-output and random-rule controls. Prove metric sensitivity by known positive fixtures. Treat all resulting apparent “intelligence” as generated, not independent agency. CPU/time limit and deterministic termination.
3. **EXP-CONSCIOUS-PRED-01:** Encode two or more *published* opposed consciousness predictions using the existing local reasoner and a manually curated, citation-bound mini-record. Blind a held-out observation, run the reasoner, then disclose; record indistinguishable predictions and abstention. Literature mapping, **not** subjective experience detection.
4. **EXP-AUGER-EXPOSURE-01:** Build frozen isotropic-sky synthetic controls and a realistic documented detector-exposure transform before downloading any observational sample. Explicitly test a cubic-anisotropy alternative; use proper selection, energy and sky coverage restrictions. Do not claim a negative result can falsify all possible simulations.
5. **EXP-STATE-CONTINUITY-01:** Snapshot/resume a harmless software agent at known deterministic checkpoints; compare outputs, memory lineage, counterfactual fork divergence and rollback behavior. Call it *functional state continuity*. No score may be reported as continuity of personal subjective experience.

All five require separate preregistration of hypotheses, immutable challenge partitions, stopping rules, falsifiers, resource budgets, and independent review before a **confirmatory** label. Before acquisition, check licensing/terms, exact dataset sizes, sample strategy, CPU/RAM/storage requirements, and the Lappy's actual available resources. Only take bounded slices; never assume a nominally free data portal is lightweight. Synthetic results alone cannot support a claim about the physical universe.

### Explicitly deferred or rejected approaches

- **No all-frequency/port-number “magic” broadcast:** software ports are ordinary routable address endpoints, not transmission frequencies. A missing recipient/channel cannot be supplied by unusual port choices [E-07,E-20].
- **No inference of a message from public random bits:** statistics can test predeclared correlation models, but unregistered patterns after searching are post-selection [E-09,E-18].
- **No Bell/entanglement messaging:** experimentally nonclassical correlations do not themselves enable controllable remote communication under established quantum protocols [E-06,E-17,E-22].
- **No quantum consciousness-transfer claim from quantum-state teleportation:** different system, scope and measurement; functional software capsule tests remain separate [E-17,E-22,E-23].
- **No forced model agreement:** shared training data or prompts cannot create independent corroboration; preserve source-family ancestry [E-19].
